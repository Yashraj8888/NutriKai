Started at a kitchen table, not a factory floor

Nutri Kai began the way a lot of good food does — with too much of it. A family batch of roasted seeds, meant for a Diwali gift box, turned into requests from neighbours, then from their neighbours. We kept the recipe the same and just made more of it.

Every sachet still goes through the same three steps it did on day one: source in small lots directly from farmers and trusted processors, roast or sort by hand in batches small enough to taste-check, and pack only after an order comes in. Nothing sits on a shelf long enough to go stale.

We're a small team working out of Bengaluru, which is also why every order still goes out over a WhatsApp message rather than an anonymous checkout page — we like knowing who we're packing for.

Why people reorder

Six habits we haven't compromised on since the first batch.

Sourced direct

We buy in small lots straight from farms and processors, skipping the chain of resellers that ages most packaged snacks.

Roasted, not fried

Every seed and nut is dry-roasted in small batches — never deep-fried — so the natural flavour stays in charge.

No additives

No preservatives, added sugar or artificial flavouring goes into anything we pack — just what's on the label.

Packed to order

Sachets are sealed once your order comes in, not months in advance in a warehouse somewhere.

FSSAI licensed

Every batch is made to FSSAI food-safety standards, with the licence number printed on every pack.

Checked by hand

Each lot is sorted and inspected by hand before packing — not left to a machine grader alone.

Get in touch

Questions about an order, bulk pricing for an office or event, or a custom mix? Message us directly — we reply on WhatsApp, usually within the day.
