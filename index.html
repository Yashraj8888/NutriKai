Why people reorder

Six habits we haven't compromised on since the first batch.

Sourced direct

We buy in small lots straight from farms and processors, skipping the chain of resellers that ages most packaged snacks.

Roasted, not fried

Every seed and nut is dry-roasted in small batches — never deep-fried — so the natural flavour stays in charge.

No additives

No preservatives, added sugar or artificial flavouring goes into anything we pack — just what's on the label.

Packed to order

Sachets are sealed once your order comes in, not months in advance in a warehouse somewhere.

FSSAI licensed

Every batch is made to FSSAI food-safety standards, with the licence number printed on every pack.

Checked by hand

Each lot is sorted and inspected by hand before packing — not left to a machine grader alone.
